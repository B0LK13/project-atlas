
## Roles

| Layer | Promise | Studio relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Coder Alpha | Never explain your project to an AI twice | Studio is a surface over that persistent brain |

| Coordination F1–F16 | Autonomous control plane (implementation layer) | Studio Mission Control consumes these primitives |
| Atlas Studio | Linux-first human workstation UX | Projection/control only; daemon remains authority |

## Non-erasure

Studio **extends** the product horizon. It does not erase Coder Alpha, rewrite
Atlas 2.x foundations, unlock Atlas-OPT, or waive independent verification.
