# AT3-070 — Surface contract (CLI/API/Web/TUI/MCP/A2A)

Isolated Atlas 3 runtime. Does not mutate certified 2.x surfaces.

- Functions: `compile_surface_contract`, `evaluate_surface_claim`
- CLI: `atlas surface-contract [--surface <id>] [--claim projection] [--transport-status <code>]`
- Surfaces: `cli`, `api` (alias `live_api`), `web`, `tui`, `mcp`, `a2a`
- SURFACE != TRUTH CORE
- TRANSPORT SUCCESS != AUTHORITY
- Surface availability != owner authorization
- Unknown surface or authority/merge/owner claim fails closed
- MERGE_AUTHORIZATION = NOT_GRANTED
