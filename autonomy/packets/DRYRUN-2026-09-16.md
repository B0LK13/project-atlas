## Internal gating found three defects in the first attempt

Both subagents ran against head `311ce7b6991ddf18e6a39e682b4a3ee45da72b67`. Their outputs are
advisory only: no verdict here is the owner's supervisor closure, and neither wrote to the repo.

| Source | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Supervisor (blocking) | The first resume fix scoped **every** rule to events after the last resume, so an unrelated `resume` erased the REDESIGN retry cap, reopened iterations closed by CONTINUE/ACCELERATE/DEFER and un-paused OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED ones. The docstring claimed the opposite. | Fixed: a resume now lifts only the `STOP` verdicts before it. Pinned by `test_resume_lifts_only_the_stop_it_follows` and `test_resume_reopens_only_the_stopped_iteration_not_its_successor`, which fail against the first attempt. |
| Supervisor (blocking) | `--no-fetch` was an unrestricted, unlogged HALT bypass on the gate itself, with no role check. | Fixed: the CLI flag is removed. `fetch=False` survives only as an in-process parameter for tests and this out-of-repo harness. Pinned by `test_the_gate_has_no_switch_that_skips_the_grant_ref_refresh`. |
| Supervisor (non-blocking) | Four required checks are declared but only their names are validated; nothing here queries their green status. | Disclosed in policy section 12.3 as unmechanized, with branch protection named as owner-held. |
| Verifier (defect) | An appended blob with an **interior blank line** passed `check_ledger_append` but `load_ledger` then rejected the stored file as tampering: a self-inflicted lockout on an append-only file. | Fixed: only the synthetic trailing empty element is skipped; any other blank line is refused. Pinned by `test_appended_blank_lines_are_refused_at_the_gate_that_produced_them` and a parity test asserting the append gate accepts exactly what `load_ledger` accepts. |

The verifier also returned NOT_CERTIFIED for a second reason: CI on `311ce7b6` had not finished
when its budget expired. That is correct procedure, and it is why no cert exists for any head here.
